Each group shall undertake one approved robotics project involving a robot with at least three degrees of freedom. Planar robots are not permitted. The project shall include, as applicable, forward kinematics, velocity kinematics, inverse kinematics, dynamic simulation, path planning, trajectory generation, and control.The project shall be carried out entirely in simulation using Scikit-Robot or MuJoCo. Built-in functions may be used for robot modelling, visualization, collision checking, physics simulation, and verification. However, algorithms that form the core learning objectives of the course, including forward and inverse kinematics, Jacobian computation, path planning, trajectory generation, and feedback control, shall be implemented by the students using basic Python, primarily NumPy and NumPy linear-algebra routines. The mathematical models and algorithms used shall be clearly explained.A suitable feedback controller shall be designed and implemented to execute the desired robot motion or task. The choice of controller, controller parameters, and tuning procedure shall be justified.Each group shall conduct at least three meaningful simulation experiments. These shall include a nominal case and at least one challenging case involving changes in initial conditions, robot parameters, disturbances, obstacles, payload, or other relevant operating conditions.The project shall include at least one meaningful comparative study, such as a comparison of two controllers, path-planning algorithms, trajectory-generation methods, inverse-kinematics methods, or parameter settings. The comparison shall be supported by appropriate quantitative performance measures.Each group shall develop and maintain its own implementation and results. Every group member shall be able to explain the mathematical formulation, algorithms, code, and simulation results, and shall be prepared to make reasonable modifications to the implementation during evaluation.The final submission shall consist of a concise technical report, complete source code with instructions for execution, relevant plots and quantitative simulation results, and a live demonstration. The report shall clearly state the problem formulation, assumptions, methodology, results, limitations, conclusions, and individual contributions of the group members.
